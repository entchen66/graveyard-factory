    About

    A free, unofficial factory planner for Graveyard Keeper 2. Draw your conveyor factory on the real factory floor, or give the planner target outputs per minute (Supply: Iron, Building Kit I, Furniture I…) and it works out how many smithies, assembly benches and kitchens you need, places them and routes the belts.

    • All 67 factory recipes, with craft times, worker talent levels and required extensions (Bellows, Hammer, Press, Auto-hammer, Drill press, Lathe…)
    • Conveyor belts, splitters, underground conveyors, chests with side filters, distribution and supply stations
    • Station layouts and extension slots as in the game; validation of blocked ports and missing extensions
    • Saves in your browser; import and export layouts as JSON

    Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with Lazy Bear Games or tinyBuild; game art and data © their owners. Source and docs on GitHub.

    Controls

    • Drag paint · Shift+drag rectangle
    • R / Shift+R rotate
    • Right-drag erase entities
    • Wheel zoom · Middle/Space+drag pan
    • Del delete selected · Esc select tool
    • Belts follow drag direction
